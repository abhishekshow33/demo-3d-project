# La Martiniere content provenance

Capture date: 21 June 2026

The redesign uses a curated local snapshot of publicly visible content from the official La Martiniere Kolkata, Boys and Girls websites. Every published content section retains a direct official source link. The source inventory is stored in `content/source-inventory.json`.

## Editorial corrections

- Standardized the motto to **Labore et Constantia** where the source displayed “Lobore”.
- Removed the Boys acting-principal biography that appeared within the Girls About page.
- Corrected spacing, punctuation and inconsistent capitalization without changing institutional claims.
- Reconciled fluctuating public pupil counts by using the count stated on each current landing page.
- Summarized award tables in the immersive panels; their official source remains linked from the complete achievement article.
- Marked courts and pools as a **reference-led virtual recreation** wherever exact architectural plans were unavailable.

## Media policy

The supplied Boys and Girls crest files are used locally as the primary branded media. Achievement frames use designed colour fields in the WebGL experience so the site does not hotlink gallery media. Official gallery imagery can be added later only with confirmed usage approval and must include source and alt-text records.
